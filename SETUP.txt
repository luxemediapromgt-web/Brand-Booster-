SETUP (live karne se pehle 3 kaam)

1) DOMAIN: index.html, sitemap.xml aur robots.txt mein "YOUR-DOMAIN.com" ko apni asli domain se replace karein
   (jaise www.brandbooster.com). Ye share preview, Google aur sitemap ke liye zaroori hai.

2) CONTACT FORM EMAIL: js/config.js mein FORM_EMAIL likha hua hai. Site live hone ke baad pehli baar form
   submit karein. Us email par FormSubmit ka "Activate" ka mail aayega, usay click karein. Uske baad har
   enquiry seedhi email par aaye gi. Email na pohonch sake to customer ka message WhatsApp par chala jata hai.

3) ANALYTICS: js/config.js mein GA_ID (Google Analytics 4, 'G-XXXXXXXXXX') aur PIXEL_ID (Meta Pixel) likh dein.
   Khali hon to kuch load nahi hota.

Hosting: poora folder kisi bhi static hosting par chalta hai (Netlify, Vercel, Cloudflare Pages, cPanel).
